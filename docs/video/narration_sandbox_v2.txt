[u3] Locked quote on the approver page
Here's the key idea. The agent may only spend at the rate a person approved.
For Globex, it asks Airwallex for a quote: a rate with an end time. That opens a separate approver page, sized for a phone.
They see what they pay, what the supplier gets, the locked rate, and a countdown.
Approve in time, and the agent may use exactly this quote. Nothing else.

The approver gets pulled into a meeting, and the clock runs out.
The tap on Approve comes too late. The app refuses. Nothing was converted, nothing was paid. The log shows zero execution calls.
So the agent asks for a fresh quote, and shows what changed. Eleven cents less this time. Either way, it won't convert without a quote a person approved.
The approver says yes to the new quote, and it goes through.
